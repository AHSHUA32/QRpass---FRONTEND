CHAPTER I
 INTRODUCTION 
Rationale of the Study 
Security plays a vital role in maintaining a safe, organized, and productive learning environment within educational institutions. As universities continue to grow in population and operational complexity, the need for efficient and reliable campus security systems becomes increasingly important. Modern campuses require effective methods for monitoring individuals and managing the movement of items within the institution to ensure safety and accountability. In this context, the integration of digital solutions has become essential in improving security operations and strengthening institutional control.
With the increasing reliance on technology in educational institutions, many security processes are still being conducted manually. Traditional methods of item registration, inspection, and verification often rely on paper-based logs and manual checking procedures. These processes are time-consuming, prone to human error, and may result in data inconsistencies, misplaced records, and delays in verification. Furthermore, manual systems make it difficult to efficiently track and validate items entering or leaving the campus, limiting the effectiveness of security personnel in ensuring safety and control.
At the University of Cebu Main Campus, stakeholders such as the Security Office, Civil Security Unit (CSU), Student Affairs Office (SAO), students, faculty members, and staff encounter challenges related to item registration and verification procedures. Issues such as slow processing of item permits, difficulty in verifying ownership of items, lack of centralized records, inefficient monitoring of inspected items, and delays in the approval and verification process between the Student Affairs Office (SAO) and Civil Security Unit (CSU) can affect campus security operations. These limitations highlight the need for a more organized, accurate, and efficient system that can support real-time verification and secure record management.

Through a web-based platform, QRpass enables faster verification processes, improved record accuracy, and easier tracking of registered items. The system aims to enhance coordination among security personnel and stakeholders while reducing manual workload and processing delays. Ultimately, QRpass is expected to improve campus security efficiency and provide a more reliable and modern approach to item management within the University of Cebu Main Campus.

Scope and Limitations of the Study
Scope
The creation of QRpass: Enhancing Campus Security Through a QR-Based Item Registration and Verification Application  is the main topic of this project. It is a web-based system intended to simplify and secure item management on the University of Cebu Main Campus. In addition to integrating role-based access, permit approval procedures, and QR scanning for campus inspections, the system allows students, teachers, staff, and security professionals to digitally register, verify, and monitor objects using QR code technology. It also includes lost and found reports, real-time monitoring, notifications, audit logs, and an administrative dashboard powered by a centralized database. The system attempts to increase operational effectiveness, accountability, transparency, and overall campus security by automating item monitoring and verification procedures.
Limitations
The system is designed exclusively for the University of Cebu Main Campus and may require modifications for use in other institutions.
The system relies on stable internet connectivity for real-time QR scanning, verification, and data synchronization.
The study does not include advanced security technologies such as biometric authentication, facial recognition, artificial intelligence-based surveillance, GPS tracking, or integration with physical security hardware such as CCTV or automated gate systems.
The study focuses on development and testing only and does not cover long-term evaluation or large-scale deployment.
Significance of the Study
The development of QRpass: Enhancing Campus Security Through a QR-Based Item Registration and Verification Application is significant as it addresses the limitations of manual and paper-based item registration and verification processes within the University of Cebu Main Campus. By introducing a centralized and automated QR-based system, the study aims to improve the efficiency, accuracy, and transparency of campus security operations.
For the Student Affairs Office (SAO), the system provides a streamlined platform for reviewing and approving item registration requests, reducing manual paperwork and improving processing efficiency in permit approval workflows.
For the Civil Security Unit (CSU), QRpass enables faster and more accurate verification of registered items through QR code scanning, improving inspection efficiency at campus entry and exit points.
For the University Administration, the system provides centralized records and real-time reports that support better decision-making, monitoring, and policy enforcement related to campus security.
For Security Personnel, QRpass simplifies daily operations by enabling faster item verification, reducing manual paperwork, and improving the accuracy of inspection and monitoring processes.
For Students, Faculty Members, and Staff, the system offers a more convenient and transparent process for registering and verifying items, reducing delays and improving communication with security offices.
For Future Researchers and Developers, this study serves as a reference for developing similar QR-based security and tracking systems, contributing to future innovations in campus security and information systems development.
Definition of Terms
Authentication – The process of verifying the identity of a user before granting access to the QRpass system.
Authorization – The process of determining the specific actions a verified user is allowed to perform within the system based on assigned roles and permissions.
Audit Log – A system-generated record that tracks all user activities such as item registration, QR scanning, verification, and system access for security, monitoring, and accountability purposes.
Centralized System – A system architecture where all data and processes are stored and managed in a single platform to ensure easier access, monitoring, and control.
Civil Security Unit (CSU) – The university unit responsible for enforcing campus security policies, managing campus entry and exit inspections, verifying registered items through QR code scanning, and ensuring safety and order within the University of Cebu Main Campus.
Item Registration – The process of encoding item details into the system and generating a unique QR code that serves as its official identification for tracking and verification.
Item Verification – The process of scanning a registered item’s QR code to confirm its validity, ownership, and registration status within the system.
Notification System – A system feature that sends real-time alerts and updates regarding item registration status, approval results, verification activities, and security-related events such as unauthorized attempts to check out registered items.
QR Code (Quick Response Code) – A two-dimensional barcode used to uniquely identify registered items and enable fast electronic scanning and verification.
Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) – A security mechanism that restricts system access based on user roles such as student, faculty, security personnel, or administrator.
Security Personnel – Authorized campus staff responsible for inspecting, scanning, and verifying registered items and enforcing campus security procedures during campus entry and exit.
Student Affairs Office (SAO) – The university office responsible for reviewing and approving item registration requests and ensuring compliance with campus policies before QR code issuance.
Web-Based System – A software application that runs on a web server and is accessed through a browser via the internet or local network.
Lost and Found Reporting – A system feature of QRpass that allows users and security personnel to report, identify, and match lost or found items with registered item records in the system to support the recovery and tracking of missing items within the campus.
