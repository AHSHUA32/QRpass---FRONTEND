CHAPTER II

REVIEW OF RELATED LITERATURE AND STUDIES

LOCAL LITERATURE

	The study created a campus-based tracking and monitoring system that uses QR code technology via a web-based platform and an Android app to manage, monitor, and secure persons’ admission onto campus. The system minimized paper-based procedures and human error while enabling effective data storage, retrieval, reporting, and real-time monitoring. Its efficacy and dependability were demonstrated by an exceptionally high level of acceptance across multiple quality parameters in an evaluation based on the ISO 25010 software quality standards. This research provides insights into how QR code technology can be used to enhance monitoring and verification procedures, demonstrating how digital solutions can improve data accuracy, operational efficiency, and security management in educational institutions.(Melanie L. and Cristina D. 2024)

In order to overcome the difficulties associated with manually recording student attendance at Pamantasan ng Lungsod ng Marikina, the research suggested a QR code-based attendance tracking system with SMS notification. It was discovered that traditional attendance procedures were ineffective, prone to mistakes, and susceptible to attendance fraud. The created system uses QR-enabled school IDs to automate attendance monitoring, maintains data in a central database, and notifies parents via SMS at the same time. This method lessens the administrative burden while increasing the precision, dependability, and effectiveness of attendance tracking. The systems through automation, real-time data processing, and increased accountability by streamlining verification and record management procedures.(Adlao et al. n.d.)

During the COVID-19 epidemic, the researchers created a QR code-based Log Monitoring System to help with contact tracking at Samar State University. In order to effectively identify those who could have been exposed to proven or suspected COVID-19 instances, the system was built to capture and monitor the daily logs of staff, students, and visitors. The system was assessed using the waterfall development paradigm in accordance with ISO 9126 quality requirements, which include functionality, dependability, usability, efficiency, maintainability, and portability. The results demonstrated that the technology successfully streamlined and enhanced the contact tracing procedure. Through precise data collection and effective verification techniques, this study highlights the benefits of QR code technology in improving monitoring, record management, and identification procedures.(Paculaba, S. Bathan, and Niego 2022)
In order to improve the precision and dependability of attendance tracking, the research created and assessed a Smart Attendance System that integrates GPS-based location verification with QR code scanning. In order to assist, avoid proxy attendance and other types of attendance fraud, the system requires students to be physically present in an allowed area and scan a QR code relevant to the session. The System captures timestamps, GPS positions, scan logs, and attendance information in real time via a web-based management platform and mobile application. The system successfully confirmed attendance, rejected invalid attempts, and had a good usability rating, according to evaluation data. The study demonstrates how real-time verification methods combined with QR code technology may enhance data integrity, operational effectiveness, and monitoring accuracy in digital management systems.(Rafael V. Diabordo et al. 2026)

To enhance visitor access control and security on school property, the research created and assessed a QR Code-Based Monitoring System for School Visitor Logs. By using a cloud-based database and a mobile application, the technology enables users to register their details and scan a QR code when they enter. Additionally, it offers administrators and security staff a web-based gateway to maintain visitor data and keep an eye on access activities. According to evaluation findings, the system while operating efficiently in terms of usability, emphasizes how QR code technology may improve record administration, monitoring, and verification procedures, improving security and operational effectiveness in educational establishments.(Gallera 2023)

FOREIGN LITERATURE
	The study created an intelligent mobile application that verifies the legitimacy of cars using distributed computing and QR code technology. The method uses a mobile device to scan a car’s QR code, extracting and verifying information against a central database to ensure authenticity. The results showed that the program was highly accurate at decoding QR codes and confirming car records, which improved the efficiency and dependability of the verification process. When compared to conventional verification methods, the system’s performance was further enhanced by the use of sophisticated image processing and machine learning algorithms. This study demonstrates how QR code-based verification systems may improve data security, accuracy, and real-time validation procedures-all crucial components of contemporary digital monitoring and management applications.(King Khalid University, Department of Management Information Systems, College of Business, 61471, Abha, Saudi et al. 2023)

As a contemporary substitute for conventional access control techniques, including physical keys, magnetic cards, and biometric systems, the research examined the creation and use of QR Code-Based Access Control Systems (QR-ACS). Results show that QR-based systems provide an affordable, adaptable, and user-friendly method of authentication, enabling rapid access verification via cellphones. In order to guarantee system efficacy, the assessment also emphasized the significance of secure QR code production, validation, and maintenance. Although QR-ACS increases operational security and efficiency, issues with system implementation, robustness, and other security vulnerabilities still exist. This study highlights the need for safe authentication and verification methods in improving corporate security procedures and illustrates the expanding use of QR code technology in access control.(Alsawi and Ibrahim 2026)

The study investigated the use of QR codes and blockchain technologies to enhance supply chain transparency and fight counterfeit goods. A blockchain database safely stores product details such as origin, manufacturer information, batch number, and manufacturing timestamps; QR codes give consumers an easy method to view and validate this data using a smartphone. The results indicate that by providing real-time access to product histories, the combination of blockchain and QR technology improves data transparency, authenticity verification, and customer confidence. This study highlights the potential of QR codes to enhance data correctness, transparency, and security in digital information management systems by demonstrating their efficacy as a trustworthy tool for tracking and verification procedures. (Jayaram et al. n.d.)

The study presented a QR Code and One-Time Password (OTP)-Based E-Authentication System to increase user authentication and system security. The system protects user credentials against unwanted access and password interception by combining QR code technology, OTPs, hashing algorithms, and two-factor authentication. The technology lowers security concerns related to conventional password-based authentication and improves the dependability of login processes by safely storing and validating authentication data. This study shows how QR code technology may enhance security, user validation, and access control procedures, highlighting the significance of secure verification and authentication measures in safeguarding digital systems.(M. A YA’A et al. 2025)

The paper introduces a QR Code-Based Visitor Management System that uses QR codes to automate registration, identification, and monitoring procedures. The solution increases operational efficiency, decreases processing time, minimizes human error, and strengthens security through real-time data tracking and reporting by substituting digital verification for manual processes. Additionally, it offers automatic alerts and centralized record management, which facilitate improved monitoring and decision-making. The ideas presented in this study offer insightful information about the use of QR code technology for safe identification, automated verification, and effective record keeping. These ideas can be modified to facilitate streamlined monitoring and control procedures in institutional settings.

RELATED STUDIES

Current systems show how QR-based solutions can reduce manual processes and human error while automating visitor logging, attendance, real-time monitoring, and centralized data management. Additionally, studies highlight how crucial user accessibility, safe authentication, and dependable data storage are to improving operational effectiveness. These technologies offer useful references for creating contemporary security systems that support efficiency, accuracy, and transparency.
